Kick-Off — solo soccer training for kids

Parents set up the space, time and gear in-app, then hand the phone over. The child picks a pro guide, follows a Duolingo-style path of short drills sized to what they actually have (a ball, a wall, ten minutes), and earns XP, streaks, badges and guide kits. Organic palette: cream ground, terracotta actions, sage pitch. Use the Tweaks panel to switch between the clickable prototype and all screens side by side.
