# Card Texts — Main Line Units 21 to 30 and All Branch Units

Companion to `path-design.md` and `cards-units-01-20.md`. This file completes the library: 10 main-line units and 18 branch units, 154 nodes in total.

## How to read this file

Same conventions as the first card file. Each unit opens with a family block whose **Picture it** and **Set up** every node inherits unless overridden. Each node has a name, a one-liner, up to three **Do this** bullets, one **Say this** cue, and a **Meta** line.

Placeholders filled at runtime:

- `{strong}` and `{weak}` → the child's dominant and other foot, as "left" or "right"
- `{name}` → the child's first name
- `{partner}` → "Dad", "Mum", the sibling's name, or "your partner", from the People row

Branch units appear on the path only when their unlock unit is complete, and they light up only when today's gear and people allow. Substitutes are listed in the family block. A node marked **solo fallback** is what the generator uses when the node above it needs a partner or a wall and neither exists.

# Part A · Main line, units 21 to 30

## Unit 21 · D10 Sidestep Feint

**Idea**: fake to one side with the whole body, take the ball the other way. No fancy footwork, just a convincing lie.
**Gear**: 1 ball, 1 marker. Small yard or bigger.

**Picture it**: {name} jogging at a marker, dipping a shoulder and stepping hard to one side as if going that way, then pushing the ball past the marker on the other side.
**Set up**: · Marker 6 big steps ahead as a pretend defender. · Ball at your feet.

### Sidestep Feint · to the {strong} side
Fake toward your {strong} side, then take the ball past on your {weak} side.
- Walk toward the marker with the ball.
- One step before it, plant your {strong} foot wide and dip your shoulder that way.
- Push the ball past the marker on the other side with the outside of your {weak} foot.
**Say this**: "Big step one way. Ball goes the other way."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 1

### Sidestep Feint · to the {weak} side
Fake toward your {weak} side, take it past on your {strong}.
- Plant your {weak} foot wide, shoulder dips.
- Push past with the outside of your {strong} foot.
- Come back and alternate sides each rep.
**Say this**: "Fake {weak}, go {strong}."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 2

### Sidestep Feint · jogging
Same feint arriving at a jog.
- Jog at the marker.
- Plant, dip, push past. Do not slow down before the plant.
- Alternate sides.
**Say this**: "Do not slow down. Plant and go."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 2

### Sidestep Feint · then accelerate
Feint, then sprint three touches past the marker.
- Plant and dip one step before the marker.
- Push the ball past and sprint after it for three quick touches.
- Stop it dead, walk back, go again.
**Say this**: "Fake, then burst!"
**Meta**: 4 min · difficulty 3

### Sidestep Feint · against me, standing still
The parent stands in for the marker.
- I stand still where the marker was.
- Come at me, plant and dip, go past the other side.
- Try to make me lean the wrong way.
**Say this**: "Sell it to me."
**Meta**: 4 min · difficulty 4 · needs parent joining

## Unit 22 · D11 Change of Pace

**Idea**: slow, slow, fast. Markers set where the speed changes. Beating people with speed changes, not tricks.
**Gear**: 1 ball, 3 markers. Small yard or bigger, medium outdoor ideal.

**Picture it**: three markers in a line, 4 big steps apart, like traffic lights. {name} dribbles slowly to the middle one and then explodes to the last.
**Set up**: · Three markers in a straight line, 4 big steps apart. · Start with the ball at the first.

### Change of Pace · slow to fast at the middle
Dribble slowly to the middle marker, then sprint to the last.
- Walk the ball to the middle marker, small touches.
- The moment you pass it, sprint to the last marker with the ball.
- Stop it dead, walk back, go again.
**Say this**: "Slow, slow, GO."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 1

### Change of Pace · stop dead, then go
Stop the ball completely at the middle marker, then sprint.
- Dribble to the middle marker and stop the ball dead with your sole.
- Count one in your head, then burst to the last marker.
- Walk back, go again with the other foot stopping.
**Say this**: "Stop. One. Go!"
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 2

### Change of Pace · fast, slow, fast
Sprint to the middle, slow right down, sprint again.
- Sprint from the first marker to the middle.
- Slow to a walk for two touches.
- Sprint to the last marker.
**Say this**: "Fast, slow, fast."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 2

### Change of Pace · on my clap
Dribble slowly and sprint the moment I clap, then slow again on the next clap.
- Dribble slowly around the space.
- Clap means sprint. Next clap means slow down.
- Keep the ball within one step even when sprinting.
**Say this**: "Clap! ... Clap!"
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 3

### Change of Pace · with slalom
Slalom through the markers slowly, then sprint out.
- Put the three markers 2 big steps apart.
- Weave through them slowly with small touches.
- Explode out of the last one and sprint 6 big steps.
**Say this**: "Careful, careful, then go!"
**Meta**: 4 min · difficulty 3

## Unit 23 · F10 Stop & Trap

**Idea**: kill a moving ball dead. The skill that makes everything else possible.
**Gear**: 1 ball. Any space.

**Picture it**: {name} rolling the ball away, chasing it, and stopping it stone dead with one foot, then doing it with a ball dropped from the hands.
**Set up**: · Ball in your hands or at your feet. · A few big steps of room in front of you.

### Stop & Trap · roll, chase, sole stop, {strong}
Roll the ball ahead, chase it, stop it dead with your {strong} sole.
- Push the ball 3 big steps ahead with any foot.
- Chase it and put the sole of your {strong} foot on top so it stops completely.
- If it rolls even a little, try softer next time.
**Say this**: "Chase it, stamp it, dead."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 1

### Stop & Trap · sole stop, {weak}
Same chase and stop with the {weak} sole.
- Roll it ahead, chase, {weak} sole on top.
- Ten times, then swap for ten.
**Say this**: "{weak} sole. Stop it still."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 1

### Stop & Trap · inside stop
Stop a rolling ball with the inside of your foot instead of the sole.
- Roll it ahead and chase.
- Turn your foot sideways and let the ball hit the inside softly. Let your foot give a little.
- Alternate feet.
**Say this**: "Soft foot. Let it kiss the inside."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 2

### Stop & Trap · toss, bounce, sole trap
Toss the ball up, let it bounce once, trap it dead under your sole.
- Toss the ball up to head height.
- Let it bounce once, then put your sole on top as it comes down.
- Alternate feet.
**Say this**: "Bounce, then stamp."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 2

### Stop & Trap · toss, bounce, inside trap
Same toss and bounce, but stop it with the inside of your foot.
- Toss up, one bounce.
- As it drops, cushion it with the inside of your foot and let it settle at your feet.
- Alternate feet.
**Say this**: "Catch it with the inside of your foot."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 3

### Stop & Trap · chest then sole
Toss the ball, cushion it on your chest, then trap it under your sole.
- Toss the ball above your head.
- Lean back a little and let it land on your chest so it drops straight down.
- Trap it with your sole as it lands.
**Say this**: "Puff your chest out. Let it drop. Stamp."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 3 · age 11+

## Unit 24 · M02 Line Steps

**Idea**: ladder patterns. Fast feet in a row of boxes. Uses a ladder if there is one, chalk or sticks if not.
**Gear**: agility ladder, or 6 to 8 sticks laid across, or chalk boxes, or the gaps between paving slabs. Ball not needed.

**Picture it**: a row of six to eight boxes on the ground in a straight line, each about a big step long, like a ladder lying flat. {name} steps through them with quick feet.
**Set up**: · Lay out the ladder, or make 6 to 8 boxes with sticks or chalk about one big step long. · Start at one end.

### Line Steps · one foot per box
Run through the boxes putting one foot in each.
- Right foot in box one, left in box two, and so on.
- Stay on your toes. Small quick steps.
- Jog back and go again. Six runs.
**Say this**: "One foot, one box. Quick!"
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 1

### Line Steps · two feet per box
Both feet land in every box.
- Right in, left in, box one. Right in, left in, box two.
- Fast but tidy. Do not skip a box.
- Six runs.
**Say this**: "In, in. In, in."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 1

### Line Steps · sideways
Face sideways and step through with both feet in each box.
- Turn so the ladder runs to your side.
- Lead foot in, trail foot in, move to the next box.
- Go back leading with the other foot.
**Say this**: "Side step, side step."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 2

### Line Steps · in-in-out
Two feet in the box, then both feet out to the sides, then in the next box.
- Both feet in box one.
- Jump both feet out either side of box two.
- Both feet in box two. Out around box three. Keep going.
**Say this**: "In, in, out, out."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 3

### Line Steps · hops
Hop through on one foot, then the other.
- Hop on your {strong} foot, one hop per box.
- Jog back. Hop through on your {weak} foot.
- Three runs each foot.
**Say this**: "Hop, hop, hop. Soft landings."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 3

### Line Steps · icky shuffle
Two feet in, one foot out to the side, alternating sides.
- Left foot in box one, right foot in box one, left foot out to the left of box two.
- Right in box two, left in box two, right out to the right of box three.
- Slow first. It takes a few tries to click.
**Say this**: "In, in, out. In, in, out."
**Meta**: 4 min · difficulty 4 · age 11+

## Unit 25 · M03 Hops

**Idea**: jumping over small things. Power in the legs, soft landings.
**Gear**: 4 to 6 small hurdles, or shoes, rolled towels, or a ball. Small yard or bigger.

**Picture it**: a row of four to six small things to jump over, each a big step apart, no higher than {name}'s knee.
**Set up**: · Lay 4 to 6 hurdles, shoes, or towels in a row, one big step apart. · Start behind the first one.

### Hops · two-footed over
Jump over each one with both feet together.
- Feet together. Jump over the first, land softly, jump the next.
- Bend your knees on every landing.
- Jog back. Six runs.
**Say this**: "Boing, boing, boing. Quiet landings."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 1

### Hops · single leg, {strong}
Hop over each one on your {strong} foot only.
- Hop on your {strong} foot over the first, land on the same foot, hop the next.
- Arms out for balance.
- Three runs, then rest.
**Say this**: "{strong} foot only. Stick the landing."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 2

### Hops · single leg, {weak}
Same on your {weak} foot.
- Hop on the {weak} foot over each one.
- Small hops are fine. Land softly.
- Three runs.
**Say this**: "{weak} foot. Balance!"
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 2

### Hops · sideways
Stand beside the row and hop sideways over each one.
- Feet together, facing sideways.
- Jump sideways over the first, then the next.
- Go back the other way.
**Say this**: "Side hop, side hop."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 3

### Hops · then sprint 5 steps
Hop the row, then sprint 5 big steps.
- Two-footed over every hurdle.
- The moment you land after the last one, sprint 5 big steps.
- Walk back. Six runs.
**Say this**: "Hop, hop, hop, GO!"
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 3

## Unit 26 · M05 Balance

**Idea**: standing on one leg and staying there, then adding a ball. Quiet, and secretly hard.
**Gear**: 1 ball optional. Any space.

**Picture it**: {name} standing like a flamingo on one leg, arms out, still as a statue.
**Set up**: · Stand on flat ground. · Ball nearby for the later nodes.

### Balance · {strong} leg, 20 seconds
Stand on your {strong} leg for 20 seconds without touching down.
- Lift your {weak} foot off the ground and hold it a little behind you.
- Arms out. Look at one spot ahead.
- Twenty seconds. Rest. Three times.
**Say this**: "Statue. Eyes on one spot."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 1

### Balance · {weak} leg, 20 seconds
Same on your {weak} leg.
- Lift the {strong} foot. Hold.
- Twenty seconds, three times.
**Say this**: "Wobbling is fine. Do not touch down."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 1

### Balance · eyes closed
One leg, eyes closed, 10 seconds.
- Stand on one leg. Get steady.
- Close your eyes and count to ten out loud.
- Swap legs.
**Say this**: "Eyes shut. Count out loud."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 2

### Balance · catch a toss
Stand on one leg and catch the ball I toss to you.
- Stand on your {strong} leg.
- I toss the ball to you gently. Catch it and toss it back without touching down.
- Ten catches, then swap legs.
**Say this**: "Catch, throw. Stay up."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 3 · needs parent joining · solo fallback: toss the ball to yourself and catch it

### Balance · tap the ball with the free foot
Stand on one leg and tap the top of the ball with the other foot.
- Ball on the ground in front of you. Stand on your {strong} leg.
- Tap the top of the ball with your {weak} foot ten times without touching down.
- Swap legs.
**Say this**: "Tap, tap, tap. One leg."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 3

## Unit 27 · D07 Cruyff Turn

**Idea**: shape to kick, then flick the ball behind the standing leg with the inside of the foot and turn. Named after the player who made it famous.
**Gear**: 1 ball, 1 marker. Small yard or bigger.

**Picture it**: {name} swinging a leg as if to pass the ball forward, but instead the inside of the foot hooks it back behind the other leg, and they turn and go.
**Set up**: · Marker 6 big steps ahead. · Ball at your feet.

### Cruyff Turn · walking, {strong}
Walk to the marker, pretend to kick with your {strong} foot, hook it behind your standing leg instead.
- Walk to the marker with the ball on your {strong} side.
- Swing your {strong} leg as if to pass forward, but hook the inside of your foot around the ball and push it behind your {weak} leg.
- Turn and walk back with it.
**Say this**: "Fake the pass, hook it behind you."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 2

### Cruyff Turn · walking, {weak}
Same turn with your {weak} foot.
- Ball on your {weak} side.
- Swing the {weak} leg, hook the ball behind the {strong} leg.
- Turn and go back.
**Say this**: "{weak} foot Cruyff. Slow is fine."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 3

### Cruyff Turn · jogging
Arrive at a jog and make the turn in one motion.
- Jog to the marker.
- Fake and hook without stopping.
- Alternate feet each rep.
**Say this**: "Do not stop. Fake, hook, go."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 3

### Cruyff Turn · on my call
Dribble anywhere and Cruyff turn when I say "Cruyff".
- Dribble around the space.
- On "Cruyff", fake the pass and hook it behind you with the nearest foot.
- Three quick touches the new way.
**Say this**: "Cruyff!"
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 4

### Cruyff Turn · into a pass
Cruyff turn, then pass to {partner} or into a wall.
- Dribble to the marker, Cruyff turn.
- Two touches, then pass to {partner} or against the wall.
- Control the return and go again.
**Say this**: "Turn, two touches, pass."
**Meta**: 4 min · difficulty 4 · needs partner or wall · solo fallback: pass to a marker

### Cruyff Turn · into a shot
Cruyff turn, then shoot at the target.
- Set the marker 8 big steps from the target, facing away from it.
- Dribble to the marker, Cruyff turn so you face the target.
- Two touches, shoot.
**Say this**: "Turn and shoot."
**Meta**: 4 min · difficulty 4 · needs target

## Unit 28 · F11 Ball Mastery Circuit

**Idea**: chain the ball mastery patterns already learned into one flowing circuit. Unlocks after units 2, 3, 6, 8, and 10.
**Gear**: 1 ball. Any space.

**Picture it**: {name} on the spot, switching from one footwork pattern to the next every 20 seconds without the ball stopping.
**Set up**: · Ball in front of you. · I will keep time and call the changes.

### Circuit · toe taps into inside touches
Twenty seconds of toe taps, then twenty seconds of inside touches, repeat.
- Toe taps for 20 seconds.
- When I say "inside", switch to inside touches without stopping the ball.
- Back to toe taps on "taps". Three rounds.
**Say this**: "Taps! ... Inside! ... Taps!"
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 2

### Circuit · add sole rolls
Three patterns now: taps, inside touches, sole rolls.
- Twenty seconds each.
- Switch on my call without the ball stopping.
- Two full rounds.
**Say this**: "Taps! Inside! Rolls!"
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 3

### Circuit · add pull-backs
Four patterns: taps, inside touches, sole rolls, pull-backs.
- Twenty seconds each, switch on my call.
- Alternate feet on the pull-backs.
- Two full rounds.
**Say this**: "Taps! Inside! Rolls! Pull-backs!"
**Meta**: 4 min · difficulty 3

### Circuit · I call them in any order
I call patterns in any order, any time.
- Start with any pattern.
- Switch to whatever I call: taps, inside, outside, rolls, pull-backs, V.
- I might call the same one twice.
**Say this**: "Rolls! ... V! ... Taps!"
**Meta**: 4 min · difficulty 4

### Circuit · 3-minute challenge
Three minutes non-stop, switching every 20 seconds, ball never stops.
- I set the timer for three minutes.
- Every 20 seconds I call a new pattern.
- If the ball rolls away, fetch it and keep going. Count how many times it escaped.
**Say this**: "Three minutes. Ball never sleeps."
**Meta**: 5 min · difficulty 4 · challenge, count entry as escapes

## Unit 29 · D12 Big Moves

**Idea**: the famous tricks. Each is a few movements chained together. Hard, showy, and the ones kids ask for. Unlocks after units 17 and 15.
**Gear**: 1 ball, 1 marker. Small yard or bigger.

Stop the ball with one sole, spin, drag it with the other sole, go.
- Walk to the marker. Put your {strong} sole on the ball and drag it back a little as you start to spin.
- Keep spinning. Put your {weak} sole on it and drag it through.
- Finish the spin facing forward and dribble on.
**Say this**: "Stamp, spin, stamp, go."
**Meta**: 4 min · difficulty 4

### Big Moves · Maradona spin, {weak}
Same spin starting with the {weak} sole.
- {weak} sole first, spin, {strong} sole drags through.
- Slow. Get the shape, then add speed.
**Say this**: "Other way round. Stamp, spin, stamp."
**Meta**: 4 min · difficulty 4

### Big Moves · Matthews, {strong}
Nudge the ball inside with the inside of the foot, then push it outside with the outside of the same foot.
- Walk to the marker. Small nudge across with the inside of your {strong} foot.
- Straight away, push it the other way with the outside of the same foot and go past.
- Two touches, same foot, opposite directions.
**Say this**: "In, then out. Same foot."
**Meta**: 4 min · difficulty 4

### Big Moves · Matthews, {weak}
Same in-out with the {weak} foot.
- Inside of the {weak} nudges in, outside of the {weak} pushes out.
- Alternate with the {strong} each rep.
**Say this**: "{weak} in, {weak} out."
**Meta**: 4 min · difficulty 4

### Big Moves · L-feint, simple
Pull back with the sole and push sideways with the inside, then go the new way.
- Approach the marker. Pull the ball back with your {strong} sole.
- Push it sideways with the inside of the same foot.
- Go past the marker on that side.
**Say this**: "Back, across, go."
**Meta**: 4 min · difficulty 3

### Big Moves · L-feint, hard
Pull back with one sole, push sideways with the other foot's inside.
- Pull the ball back with your {strong} sole.
- Push it across with the inside of your {weak} foot.
- Take it past with the {strong}. Then mirror it.
**Say this**: "Pull, switch feet, push."
**Meta**: 4 min · difficulty 4

### Big Moves · 90 angle cut
Sharp cut with the outside of the foot to go sideways at a right angle.
- Dribble at the marker.
- One step before, chop the ball hard sideways with the outside of your {strong} foot so it goes at a right angle.
- Follow it with three fast touches. Then the {weak}.
**Say this**: "Chop it square. Go sideways."
**Meta**: 4 min · difficulty 4

### Big Moves · kick-roll
Fake a kick, then roll the ball sideways with the sole of the same foot.
- Swing your {strong} foot as if to shoot.
- Instead, land the sole on the ball and roll it sideways across your body.
- Take it with the {weak} and go. Then mirror.
**Say this**: "Fake the shot, roll it away."
**Meta**: 4 min · difficulty 4

### Big Moves · Beckenbauer turn
Step over the ball forward with one foot, then turn and take it back with the inside of the other.
- Dribble to the marker. Step past the ball with your {strong} foot as if you are leaving it.
- Turn your body and take the ball back the way you came with the inside of your {weak} foot.
- Then mirror.
**Say this**: "Step past it, turn, take it back."
**Meta**: 4 min · difficulty 4

### Big Moves · Puskas drag
Drag the ball across your body with the sole and go the other way.
- Dribble to the marker at a walk.
- Put your {strong} sole on the ball and drag it all the way across to your {weak} side.
- Take it with the outside of the {weak} and go. Then mirror.
**Say this**: "Drag it right across."
**Meta**: 4 min · difficulty 4

### Big Moves · any two chained
Chain two of the moves above before passing the marker.
- Pick two moves. Do the first three steps before the marker, the second one step before.
- Go past. Come back and pick two different ones.
- Walk them first, then jog.
**Say this**: "Two tricks, then go."
**Meta**: 5 min · difficulty 5

### Big Moves · against me, standing still
The parent replaces the marker.
- I stand still where the marker was.
- Any move you like, one step before me. Go past.
- Tell me which move you are going to do before you start. Then do it well.
**Say this**: "Show me the Maradona."
**Meta**: 5 min · difficulty 5 · needs parent joining

## Unit 30 · D13 Mouse Hole

**Idea**: dribble around a circle of markers, cut into the middle, play the ball through a tiny gate. Adapted for one to four players.
**Gear**: 1 ball, 8 markers. Small yard or bigger.

**Picture it**: six markers in a circle about 8 big steps across, like a clock face, and two markers in the very middle making a gate one big step wide. {name} dribbles round the outside and darts in to play the ball through the gate.
**Set up**: · Six markers in a circle, 8 big steps across. · Two markers in the centre, one big step apart, as the mouse hole.

### Mouse Hole · pass through the gate
Dribble around the circle, cut in, pass the ball through the mouse hole.
- Dribble around the outside of the circle.
- When you reach any marker, cut in toward the middle and pass the ball through the gate.
- Run through, collect it, back on the circle. Ten times.
**Say this**: "Round the outside, in to the hole."
**Meta**: 4 min · difficulty 2

### Mouse Hole · dribble through the gate
Same, but dribble the ball through the gate instead of passing it.
- Dribble round, cut in.
- Dribble through the mouse hole with small touches. Do not touch the markers.
- Back out to the circle. Ten times.
**Say this**: "Thread it through. Do not touch the sides."
**Meta**: 4 min · difficulty 3

### Mouse Hole · timed laps
How many times through the mouse hole in 90 seconds?
- I start the timer.
- Dribble round, in, through the hole, back out. Every time through counts.
- Stop at time. Beat it next session.
**Say this**: "Ninety seconds. Go!"
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 3 · challenge, count entry

### Mouse Hole · two players, opposite directions
Two players dribble round in opposite directions and take turns through the hole.
- One goes clockwise, one anticlockwise, each with a ball.
- Only one player through the hole at a time. Look before you cut in.
- Count your own goes.
**Say this**: "Look up. Do not crash."
**Meta**: 4 min · difficulty 3 · needs 2 players, 2 balls

### Mouse Hole · four players, miss means wait
Four players, and a miss costs a five-second wait.
- Everyone dribbles round with a ball.
- Take turns through the hole. If your ball touches a marker or misses, stand still for five seconds.
- Most successful goes in 2 minutes wins.
**Say this**: "Miss and you freeze."
**Meta**: 5 min · difficulty 4 · needs 3 to 4 players, one ball each

## Branch P01 · Wall Pass

**Idea**: pass into the wall, control the return, pass again. The wall never gets tired.
**Gear**: 1 ball, a wall or fence or {partner}. Small yard or bigger. Unlocks after unit 3.

**Picture it**: {name} standing 4 big steps from a wall, passing the ball against it and controlling the rebound, over and over.
**Set up**: · Stand 4 big steps from the wall. · Ball at your feet. If {partner} is the wall, {partner} passes it straight back each time.

### Wall Pass · inside, {strong}, two-touch
Pass with the inside of your {strong} foot, control the return with one touch, pass again.
- Pass into the wall with the inside of your {strong} foot.
- Control the rebound with one touch, any foot.
- Pass again. Twenty passes.
**Say this**: "Pass, control, pass."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 1

### Wall Pass · inside, {weak}, two-touch
Same with the inside of your {weak} foot.
- Every pass with the {weak}. Control with any foot.
- Twenty passes.
**Say this**: "{weak} foot passes. Follow through."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 2

### Wall Pass · alternating feet
Pass {strong}, control, pass {weak}, control.
- Pass with the {strong}, control the return.
- Pass with the {weak}, control.
- Keep alternating. Twenty passes.
**Say this**: "{strong}, then {weak}."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 2

### Wall Pass · one-touch, {strong}
Pass the rebound straight back without controlling it first.
- Pass into the wall with the {strong}.
- Hit the rebound first time straight back.
- Count how many in a row before it gets away.
**Say this**: "No control. Straight back."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 3

### Wall Pass · one-touch, {weak}
One-touch passing with your {weak} foot.
- Every rebound straight back with the {weak}.
- Stand a step closer if it helps.
- Count your best run.
**Say this**: "{weak} foot, first time."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 3

### Wall Pass · outside foot
Pass into the wall with the outside of your foot.
- Turn your foot in and strike the ball with the outside, near the little toe.
- Control the return, pass again. Ten with each foot.
**Say this**: "Little toe pass."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 3

### Wall Pass · laces drive from 6 steps
Step back and hit a firmer pass with your laces.
- Stand 6 big steps from the wall.
- Toes down, hit the middle of the ball with your laces, low and firm.
- Control the return. Ten each foot.
**Say this**: "Toes down. Firm and low."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 3
