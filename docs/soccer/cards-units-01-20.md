# Card Texts — Main Line Units 1 to 20

Companion to `path-design.md`. This file holds the on-screen copy for every node in the first twenty main-line units, 135 nodes in total.

## How to read this file

Each unit opens with a **family block**: the one-line idea, the gear, and a shared **Picture it** and **Set up**. Every node in the unit inherits those two blocks unless it has its own. On screen, the app always renders the full card: name, one-liner, Picture it, Set up, Do this, Say this, timer.

Each node lists:

- **Name** in the form `Family · Variant`
- **One-liner**: what the child does, in one sentence
- **Do this**: up to three bullets, each starting with a verb, written for the parent to read aloud
- **Say this**: one cue in quotes for the child's ear
- **Meta**: timer in minutes, difficulty 1 to 5, age tag if not 8+

Placeholders the app fills at runtime from the child's profile:

- `{strong}` → "right" or "left", the dominant foot
- `{weak}` → the other foot
- `{name}` → the child's first name

Distances are always in big steps. A big step is roughly one metre for an adult.

Easier and Harder chips load the previous and next node in the same unit. The first node's Easier and the last node's Harder are disabled.

## Unit 1 · F01 Ball Circles

**Idea**: hands, not feet. The ball travels around the body with straight arms while the body stays still. Warms up the shoulders and gets the ball into the hands before it goes to the feet.
**Gear**: 1 ball. Any space.

**Picture it**: {name} standing still with the ball held in both hands, like they are about to throw it in.
**Set up**: · Stand anywhere with the ball in both hands. · Feet a little wider than the hips.

### Ball Circles · around the waist
Pass the ball from hand to hand around your waist without dropping it.
- Hold the ball at your belly button with both hands.
- Pass it behind your back to the other hand, then round the front again.
- Go slowly first, then a little faster each lap.
**Say this**: "The ball moves. Your hips stay still."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 1

### Ball Circles · around the knees
Circle the ball around both knees with straight arms.
- Bend your knees a little and hold the ball in front of them.
- Pass it hand to hand behind your knees and back to the front.
- Keep your arms long. Only your hands should move.
**Say this**: "Long arms. Ball hugs the knees."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 1

### Ball Circles · figure-eight through the legs
Weave the ball between your legs in a figure-eight.
- Stand with feet wide apart and knees bent.
- Pass the ball around your {strong} leg, through the gap, then around your {weak} leg.
- Keep going without stopping. It should draw an 8 on the ground.
**Say this**: "Round one leg, through the middle, round the other."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 2

### Ball Circles · switch on my call
Circle the ball around the waist and change direction every time the parent says "switch".
- Start circling the ball around your waist.
- When I say "switch", go the other way straight away.
- Do not stop the ball to switch. Just reverse.
**Say this**: "Switch!"
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 2

### Ball Circles · walking forward
Circle the ball around your waist while walking slowly forward.
- Start circling around the waist.
- Walk forward 8 big steps, circling the whole way.
- Turn around and come back.
**Say this**: "Feet slow. Hands fast."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 2

### Ball Circles · walking backward
Circle the ball around your knees while walking slowly backward.
- Bend your knees and start circling around them.
- Walk backward 8 big steps. Look over your shoulder now and then.
- Turn and come back the same way.
**Say this**: "Small steps. Do not rush."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 3

## Unit 2 · F02 Toe Taps

**Idea**: alternate feet tapping the top of the ball, light and quick. The ball does not move. Builds rhythm and the habit of small, soft touches.
**Gear**: 1 ball. Any space.

**Picture it**: the ball sits still on the ground and {name} hops from foot to foot, touching the top of it with the toes each time, like a tiny dance.
**Set up**: · Put the ball on the ground in front of you. · Stand close, arms out a little for balance.

### Toe Taps · slow, count out loud
Tap the top of the ball with one foot, then the other, counting each tap.
- Touch the top of the ball with your {strong} toes, then with your {weak} toes.
- Count out loud: one, two, three, up to twenty.
- Land softly. The ball should not roll away.
**Say this**: "Soft feet. Like tapping a sleeping cat."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 1

### Toe Taps · fast, 30 seconds
Tap as quickly as you can for 30 seconds while the ball stays still.
- Start tapping, {strong} then {weak}, as fast as you can.
- Go for 30 seconds, rest for 30, then again.
- Stay on the balls of your feet. Do not let your heels sink.
**Say this**: "Quick, quick, quick. Stay light."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 2

### Toe Taps · {weak} foot leads
Tap two times with the {weak} foot for every one with the {strong} foot.
- Tap {weak}, {weak}, {strong}. Then again.
- Keep the rhythm steady, like a drum.
- Do 20, rest, and repeat.
**Say this**: "{weak}, {weak}, {strong}. Feel the beat."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 2

### Toe Taps · walking forward
Keep tapping while the ball rolls slowly forward with you.
- Start tapping on the spot.
- Let each tap nudge the ball a tiny bit forward.
- Move 6 big steps forward without losing the rhythm.
**Say this**: "Nudge it. Do not kick it."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 3

### Toe Taps · walking backward
Tap the ball so it rolls slowly back toward you as you walk backward.
- Start tapping on the spot.
- Tap the top slightly toward you so the ball follows you back.
- Move 6 big steps backward, then come forward again.
**Say this**: "Pull it home with the taps."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 3

### Toe Taps · 30-second challenge
Count how many taps you can do in 30 seconds.
- I will start the timer and count your taps.
- Every touch of the ball counts as one.
- Try to beat your number next time.
**Say this**: "Ready. Fast feet. Go!"
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 3 · challenge, count entry

## Unit 3 · F03 Inside Touches

**Idea**: the ball goes side to side between the insides of both feet. Soft touches, bent knees. The most-used touch in soccer.
**Gear**: 1 ball. Any space.

**Picture it**: {name} standing with feet apart, the ball rolling gently from the inside of one foot to the inside of the other, like a slow pendulum.
**Set up**: · Ball on the ground between your feet. · Knees bent, back a little bent, eyes on the ball for now.

### Inside Touches · standing, slow
Roll the ball from the inside of one foot to the inside of the other, slowly.
- Push the ball across with the inside of your {strong} foot.
- Catch it with the inside of your {weak} foot and push it back.
- Keep it to one soft touch each side.
**Say this**: "Inside, inside. Nice and calm."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 1

### Inside Touches · standing, quick
Same touch, faster, with the ball staying under you.
- Start slow, then speed up every ten touches.
- Keep the ball between your feet. If it escapes, slow down.
- Bounce on your toes between touches.
**Say this**: "Quicker now. Small touches."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 2

### Inside Touches · moving forward
Keep the side-to-side touches going while moving slowly forward.
- Start the touches on the spot.
- Angle each touch slightly forward so you both creep ahead.
- Move 8 big steps, turn, come back.
**Say this**: "Zig, zag, zig, zag, forward."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 2

### Inside Touches · moving backward
Keep the touches going while stepping slowly backward.
- Start on the spot.
- Angle each touch slightly toward you and step back as you go.
- Move 8 big steps back. Look over your shoulder once.
**Say this**: "Bring it with you."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 3

### Inside Touches · {weak} foot twice
Two touches with the {weak} foot for every one with the {strong} foot.
- {weak}, {weak}, {strong}. Then repeat.
- The second {weak} touch is a small tap to set the ball for the next one.
- Keep the rhythm even.
**Say this**: "Two on the {weak}, one on the {strong}."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 3

### Inside Touches · one touch per step, jogging
Jog forward taking one inside touch per step, alternating feet.
- Jog slowly, ball at your feet.
- Every step, take one inside touch with the foot that lands.
- Go 10 big steps, turn, come back.
**Say this**: "Step touch, step touch. Like a rhythm."
**Meta**: 4 min · difficulty 4

## Unit 4 · D01 Straight Dribble

**Idea**: two markers, one straight line, one named part of the foot. Teaches how each surface moves the ball differently.
**Gear**: 1 ball, 2 markers. Small yard or bigger.

**Picture it**: two markers in a straight line, 8 big steps apart, like the two ends of a short runway. {name} dribbles from one to the other and back.
**Set up**: · Put a marker down. Walk 8 big steps and put the other one down. · Start with the ball at one marker.

### Straight Dribble · inside, {strong} foot
Dribble to the far marker and back using only the inside of your {strong} foot.
- Push the ball forward with the inside of your {strong} foot.
- Take a touch every one or two steps. Do not let it run away.
- At the marker, turn around and come back the same way.
**Say this**: "Little pushes. Keep it close."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 1

### Straight Dribble · inside, {weak} foot
Same run, only the inside of your {weak} foot.
- Push with the inside of your {weak} foot only.
- Slow down if the ball gets more than one step away.
- There and back is one lap. Do as many as you can.
**Say this**: "{weak} foot only. It is allowed to be wobbly."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 2

### Straight Dribble · outside, {strong} foot
Dribble using only the outside of your {strong} foot.
- Turn your {strong} foot slightly inward and push with the outside edge, near the little toe.
- Small touches, one per step.
- There and back is one lap.
**Say this**: "Little toe pushes it."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 2

### Straight Dribble · outside, {weak} foot
Same run, only the outside of your {weak} foot.
- Push with the outside of your {weak} foot.
- Keep your body low and your knees bent.
- There and back is one lap.
**Say this**: "Outside of the {weak}. Stay low."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 2

### Straight Dribble · laces, small touches
Dribble with the top of your foot, the laces, using small touches.
- Point your toes down and push the ball with your laces.
- Keep the touches small. It is easy to hit it too far with laces.
- Alternate feet if you like.
**Say this**: "Toes down. Tiny pushes."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 2

### Straight Dribble · alternate feet every touch
Dribble there and back changing feet on every single touch.
- {strong} touch, {weak} touch, {strong}, {weak}.
- Any part of the foot is fine. The rule is: never the same foot twice.
- Go a little faster each lap.
**Say this**: "Switch, switch, switch."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 3

### Straight Dribble · head up, count my fingers
Dribble while looking up to count the fingers I hold up.
- Dribble slowly to the far marker.
- Every few touches I hold up some fingers. Call the number out loud.
- Look down only to find the ball again.
**Say this**: "Eyes up. How many?"
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 3

### Straight Dribble · running pace
Dribble at running speed with the ball never more than one step ahead.
- Run to the far marker with the ball. Take a touch every step or two.
- If the ball gets away, you are going too fast. Slow down a notch.
- Stop the ball dead at the marker, then go back.
**Say this**: "Fast feet, but the ball stays your friend."
**Meta**: 4 min · difficulty 4

## Unit 5 · M01 Quick Feet Square

**Idea**: a small square of markers and quick feet. Builds coordination and the ability to change direction fast. No ball at first.
**Gear**: 4 markers. Ball optional. Any space bigger than tiny indoor.

**Picture it**: four markers making a square, each side 2 big steps long, like a small hopscotch box with only four corners.
**Set up**: · Put 4 markers down as the corners of a square with sides 2 big steps long. · Stand at one corner facing across the square.

### Quick Feet Square · forward and back
Run forward to the far side and back to your corner, over and over.
- Run 2 big steps forward to the far side of the square.
- Run backward to your corner. Do not turn around.
- Go for 30 seconds, rest, repeat.
**Say this**: "Forward, back. Quick feet, small steps."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 1

### Quick Feet Square · sideways shuffles
Shuffle sideways along one side of the square and back.
- Face across the square. Shuffle sideways to the next corner.
- Shuffle back. Do not cross your feet.
- Go for 30 seconds, rest, repeat.
**Say this**: "Feet apart. Do not let them touch."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 1

### Quick Feet Square · in and out on the diagonals
Sprint to the middle of the square and back out to each corner in turn.
- Start at a corner. Sprint to the middle.
- Run backward out to the next corner along.
- Keep going around all four corners.
**Say this**: "In to the middle, out to the corner."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 2

### Quick Feet Square · around the outside
Run around the outside of the square as fast as you can, tight to the corners.
- Run around the square, staying close to each marker.
- Do two laps one way, then two laps the other way.
- Bend your knees at the corners so you do not skid.
**Say this**: "Hug the corners."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 2

### Quick Feet Square · all four with the ball
Do the same four patterns, but now the ball comes with you.
- Forward and back for 30 seconds, dribbling.
- Sideways for 30 seconds, rolling the ball with the inside of your feet.
- Then diagonals, then around the outside. Small touches.
**Say this**: "Same feet, ball comes too."
**Meta**: 5 min · difficulty 3

## Unit 6 · F05 Sole Rolls

**Idea**: the bottom of the foot drags and rolls the ball. The foot never kicks it. Teaches control at close range.
**Gear**: 1 ball. Any space.

**Picture it**: {name} standing over the ball with one foot resting on top of it, like a foot on a skateboard, rolling it slowly around.
**Set up**: · Ball on the ground. · Stand with the ball just in front of your {strong} foot.

### Sole Rolls · sideways, {strong} foot
Roll the ball sideways across your body with the sole of your {strong} foot.
- Put the sole of your {strong} foot on top of the ball.
- Roll it across in front of you toward your {weak} foot.
- Stop it with the inside of your {weak} foot and roll it back with the sole.
**Say this**: "Roll it, do not kick it."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 1

### Sole Rolls · sideways, {weak} foot
Same roll, but with the sole of your {weak} foot.
- Sole of your {weak} foot on the ball.
- Roll across toward your {strong} foot, stop it, roll back.
- Slow is fine. Smooth matters more than fast.
**Say this**: "Smooth roll with the {weak} foot."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 2

### Sole Rolls · forward and pull back, {strong} foot
Roll the ball forward with the sole, then drag it back.
- Sole of your {strong} foot on top of the ball.
- Roll it forward one big step, keeping your foot on it.
- Drag it straight back to where it started.
**Say this**: "Push it out, pull it home."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 1

### Sole Rolls · forward and pull back, {weak} foot
Same forward roll and drag back with the {weak} foot.
- Sole of your {weak} foot on the ball.
- Roll forward one step, drag it back.
- Ten times, then swap feet for ten to rest.
**Say this**: "Out and home. {weak} foot."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 2

### Sole Rolls · across and stop with the other foot
Roll across with one sole and trap it dead with the other foot.
- Roll the ball across with the sole of your {strong} foot.
- Stop it dead by putting the sole of your {weak} foot on top.
- Now roll it back with the {weak} sole and stop it with the {strong}.
**Say this**: "Roll, stop. Roll, stop."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 2

### Sole Rolls · L-roll, {strong} foot
Pull the ball back with the sole, then push it sideways with the inside of the same foot, drawing an L.
- Sole of your {strong} foot on the ball. Drag it straight back toward you.
- Without putting your foot down, push it sideways with the inside of the same foot.
- Take a step to it and do it again. The ball draws an L on the ground each time.
**Say this**: "Back, then across. Draw the L."
**Meta**: 4 min · difficulty 3

### Sole Rolls · L-roll, {weak} foot
The same L with your {weak} foot.
- Sole of the {weak} foot drags the ball back.
- Inside of the {weak} foot pushes it sideways.
- Slow it down until the shape is clean, then speed up.
**Say this**: "L with the {weak} foot. Clean shape."
**Meta**: 4 min · difficulty 3

## Unit 7 · D04 Simple Turn

**Idea**: dribble to a marker, hook the ball around, come back the way you came. The first real turn.
**Gear**: 1 ball, 1 marker. Small yard or bigger.

**Picture it**: one marker 6 big steps ahead. {name} dribbles up to it, wraps a foot around the ball to turn it, and dribbles back to where they started.
**Set up**: · Put a marker 6 big steps in front of you. · Start with the ball at your feet.

### Simple Turn · inside hook, {strong} foot
Dribble to the marker and hook the ball back with the inside of your {strong} foot.
- Dribble slowly to the marker.
- Reach past the ball with your {strong} foot and hook it back toward you with the inside.
- Turn your body with it and dribble back to the start.
**Say this**: "Reach past it, hook it back."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 1

### Simple Turn · inside hook, {weak} foot
Same turn with the inside of your {weak} foot.
- Dribble to the marker.
- Hook the ball back with the inside of your {weak} foot.
- Come back to the start and go again.
**Say this**: "{weak} foot hook."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 2

### Simple Turn · outside hook, {strong} foot
Hook the ball back with the outside of your {strong} foot.
- Dribble to the marker with the ball slightly to your {strong} side.
- Wrap the outside of your {strong} foot around the far side of the ball and drag it back.
- Turn and dribble home.
**Say this**: "Little toe wraps round. Pull it back."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 2

### Simple Turn · outside hook, {weak} foot
Same outside hook with the {weak} foot.
- Ball slightly to your {weak} side as you arrive.
- Outside of the {weak} foot wraps around and drags it back.
- Dribble home. Try to make the turn one smooth movement.
**Say this**: "Outside of the {weak}. One movement."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 3

### Simple Turn · turn on my call
Dribble anywhere, and turn with any hook the moment the parent says "turn".
- Dribble around the space slowly.
- When I say "turn", hook the ball back and go the other way straight away.
- Use whichever foot is nearest the ball.
**Say this**: "Turn!"
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 3

### Simple Turn · two turns back to back
Turn at the marker, take two touches, then turn again straight away.
- Dribble to the marker and hook it back.
- Take two touches, then hook it again so you face the marker.
- Dribble to the marker and repeat.
**Say this**: "Turn, two touches, turn again."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 3

### Simple Turn · three fast touches out
Turn at the marker and burst away with three fast touches.
- Dribble to the marker at a jog. Hook it back.
- The moment you turn, take three quick touches as fast as you can.
- Slow down, walk back, go again.
**Say this**: "Turn and go, go, go!"
**Meta**: 4 min · difficulty 4

## Unit 8 · F04 Outside Touches

**Idea**: push the ball out with the outside of one foot, bring it back with the inside of the same foot. Teaches the outside of the foot, which most kids never use.
**Gear**: 1 ball. Any space.

**Picture it**: {name} standing on one leg a little, the other foot pushing the ball out to the side and pulling it back, like a windscreen wiper.
**Set up**: · Ball on the ground beside your {strong} foot. · Stand tall, arms out for balance.

### Outside Touches · {strong} foot
Push the ball out with the outside of your {strong} foot and bring it back with the inside.
- Push the ball a small step sideways with the outside of your {strong} foot.
- Bring it back with the inside of the same foot.
- Out, in, out, in. Twenty times, then rest.
**Say this**: "Out with the little toe, in with the big toe."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 1

### Outside Touches · {weak} foot
Same wiper motion with your {weak} foot.
- Ball beside your {weak} foot.
- Out with the outside, in with the inside.
- Hop on your standing foot if it helps you balance.
**Say this**: "Windscreen wiper. {weak} foot."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 2

### Outside Touches · alternating feet
Out and in with one foot, then pass it across and do the other foot.
- Out and in with your {strong} foot.
- Push it across to your {weak} foot with the inside.
- Out and in with the {weak}, then back across.
**Say this**: "Out, in, across. Out, in, across."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 2

### Outside Touches · walking forward
Keep the out-and-in going while walking slowly forward.
- Start the out-and-in on the spot.
- Angle the "in" touch slightly forward so the ball creeps ahead.
- Walk 8 big steps, turn, and come back with the other foot.
**Say this**: "Wipers on, walking forward."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 3

### Outside Touches · around a marker
Circle a marker using only outside touches of one foot.
- Put a marker down. Stand beside it with the ball on the outside.
- Walk around the marker pushing the ball with the outside of your outside foot only.
- Two laps one way with the {strong}, then two the other way with the {weak}.
**Say this**: "Outside foot pushes you round."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 3 · needs 1 marker

## Unit 9 · D02 Gates

**Idea**: four small gates in a zigzag. Through every gate with as few touches as possible. Adapted from a classic course drill.
**Gear**: 1 ball, 8 markers. Small yard or bigger.

**Picture it**: four little doorways made of two markers each, zigzagging across the yard like stepping stones. Each doorway is one big step wide. The doorways are 4 big steps apart.
**Set up**: · Make 4 gates, 2 markers each, 1 big step apart. · Place them in a zigzag, 4 big steps between gates.

### Gates · wide gates, any foot
Dribble through gate 1, 2, 3, 4 with any part of either foot.
- Start behind gate 1. Dribble through it, then to gate 2, and on to 4.
- Any foot, any touch. Just get through every gate.
- Turn around at gate 4 and come back through them all.
**Say this**: "Through every doorway."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 1 · gates 6 big steps apart for this node

### Gates · alternate feet each gate
Go through gate 1 with one foot and gate 2 with the other, and so on.
- Through gate 1 with your {strong} foot.
- Through gate 2 with your {weak} foot. Keep alternating.
- Come back and start with the {weak} this time.
**Say this**: "Change feet at every gate."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 2

### Gates · inside foot only
Use only the insides of your feet, both feet allowed.
- Every touch is with the inside of a foot.
- Steer with little inside pushes to line up each gate.
- Through all four, back through all four.
**Say this**: "Big toe side only."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 2

### Gates · outside foot only
Use only the outsides of your feet.
- Every touch is with the outside of a foot.
- It will feel odd at first. Take smaller touches.
- Through all four, back through all four.
**Say this**: "Little toe side only. Slow is fine."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 3

### Gates · {weak} foot only
Every touch with your {weak} foot, any surface.
- Only your {weak} foot touches the ball.
- Inside, outside, or laces, your choice.
- Through all four, back through all four.
**Say this**: "{weak} foot only. Look at the next gate."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 3

### Gates · one touch between gates
Take only one touch between each gate.
- Push the ball through gate 1 with one touch that carries it to gate 2.
- One more touch through gate 2 to gate 3. And so on.
- If you need two touches, that is fine. Try for one next time.
**Say this**: "One touch, next gate. One touch, next gate."
**Meta**: 4 min · difficulty 4

### Gates · both directions
Go through each gate, turn, and come back through it before moving to the next.
- Through gate 1, hook the ball round, back through gate 1.
- Now on to gate 2. Through, turn, back through.
- All four gates, then rest.
**Say this**: "Through, turn, back through."
**Meta**: 4 min · difficulty 4

### Gates · 60-second count
Count how many gates you can get through in 60 seconds.
- I start the timer. You go through as many gates as you can, any order.
- Every gate you pass through counts as one.
- Stop when I call time. Try to beat your number next time.
**Say this**: "Sixty seconds. Go!"
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 4 · challenge, count entry

### Gates · two-player race
Two players race on mirrored courses.
- Set up a second zigzag next to the first, the mirror image.
- Both start on "go". First through all four gates and back wins.
- Play best of three.
**Say this**: "Ready, set, go!"
**Meta**: 4 min · difficulty 4 · needs 2 players and 16 markers

## Unit 10 · F06 Pull-Backs

**Idea**: sole pulls the ball back, then the same foot pushes it forward again. The basic escape move.
**Gear**: 1 ball. Any space.

**Picture it**: {name} rolling the ball toward themselves with the bottom of one foot, then pushing it away again with the same foot, as if pulling a drawer open and shut.
**Set up**: · Ball one small step in front of you. · Stand with weight on your standing leg.

### Pull-Backs · {strong} foot
Pull the ball back with the sole of your {strong} foot, then push it forward with the same foot.
- Sole of your {strong} foot on top of the ball. Drag it back toward you.
- Push it forward again with your laces or inside.
- Back, forward. Twenty times.
**Say this**: "Drawer open, drawer shut."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 1

### Pull-Backs · {weak} foot
Same pull-back and push with your {weak} foot.
- Sole of your {weak} foot drags the ball back.
- Push it forward with the same foot.
- Twenty times, then rest.
**Say this**: "{weak} foot. Back and forward."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 2

### Pull-Backs · alternating
Pull back with one foot, push forward, then the other foot.
- {strong} foot pulls back and pushes forward.
- {weak} foot pulls back and pushes forward.
- Keep it flowing without stopping.
**Say this**: "{strong} pull, {weak} pull."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 2

### Pull-Backs · pull back, push inside, {strong} foot
Pull back with the sole, then push the ball sideways with the inside of the same foot.
- Drag the ball back with the sole of your {strong} foot.
- Before your foot touches the ground, push the ball across your body with the inside.
- Step to it and go again.
**Say this**: "Pull back, then inside across."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 3

### Pull-Backs · pull back, push outside, {strong} foot
Pull back with the sole, then push the ball away with the outside of the same foot.
- Drag back with the sole of your {strong} foot.
- Push the ball out to your {strong} side with the outside of the same foot.
- Step to it and go again.
**Say this**: "Pull back, then little toe out."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 3

### Pull-Backs · inside and outside, {weak} foot
The last two moves, both with your {weak} foot.
- Pull back with the {weak} sole, push across with the inside. Five times.
- Pull back with the {weak} sole, push out with the outside. Five times.
- Repeat until the timer ends.
**Say this**: "{weak} foot. Inside five, outside five."
**Meta**: 4 min · difficulty 3

### Pull-Backs · into a 180 turn
Pull the ball back and spin all the way around to face the other way.

## Unit 14 · D03 Slalom

**Idea**: markers in a line, weave through them, sprint back. Close control at speed.
**Gear**: 1 ball, 4 to 6 markers. Small yard or bigger.

**Picture it**: a straight line of markers, 3 big steps between each, like lamp posts along a road. {name} weaves in and out of them with the ball and jogs back down the side.
**Set up**: · Put 4 to 6 markers in a straight line, 3 big steps apart. · Start with the ball behind the first one.

### Slalom · {strong} foot, inside and outside
Weave through the markers using only your {strong} foot.
- Push the ball past the first marker with the inside of your {strong} foot.
- Push it back the other way past the next marker with the outside of the same foot.
- Inside, outside, inside, outside to the end. Jog back down the side.
**Say this**: "Inside, outside, inside, outside."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 1

### Slalom · {weak} foot only
Weave through with only your {weak} foot.
- Inside of the {weak} past one marker, outside past the next.
- Go slowly. Getting round every marker matters more than speed.
- Jog back and go again.
**Say this**: "{weak} foot weave."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 2

### Slalom · alternate feet each marker
Push past each marker with a different foot.
- Inside of the {strong} past the first, inside of the {weak} past the second.
- Keep switching. Both insides only.
- Jog back and go again.
**Say this**: "{strong}, {weak}, {strong}, {weak}."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 2

### Slalom · tight, 2 big steps
Same weave with the markers closer together.
- Move the markers to 2 big steps apart.
- Weave through with any feet. Touches must be smaller now.
- Jog back and go again.
**Say this**: "Tiny touches. Tight turns."
**Meta**: 3 min · difficulty 3 · markers 2 big steps apart

### Slalom · timed
Weave through and back as fast as you can while I time it.
- Start at the first marker. I say go and start the timer.

- I stop the timer when you cross the start. Beat it next time.
**Say this**: "Go! Fast but clean."

### Slalom · then sprint with the ball

- Stop it dead, walk back, go again.
**Say this**: "Weave, then explode!"
**Meta**: 4 min · difficulty 4

### Slalom · two-player relay
Two players take turns. One weaves down and back, then the other goes.
- Player one weaves down and back, then passes to player two.
- Player two weaves down and back. That is one round.
- Count how many rounds in 2 minutes.
**Say this**: "Go! Tag your partner with a pass."
**Meta**: 4 min · difficulty 3 · needs 2 players

- Every card was written from scratch. The site was used for the idea and the order of variants only.
- Timers are defaults. The generator may shorten a node to 2 minutes in a 5-minute session.
- Nodes marked "needs parent joining" or "needs 2 players" are shown gear-blocked when the People row says the child is alone. Their sibling is the previous node in the same unit.
- Nodes marked "challenge, count entry" are the ones the generator prefers for the challenge slot.
- "Hand's width" and "small step" are used for distances under one big step.
